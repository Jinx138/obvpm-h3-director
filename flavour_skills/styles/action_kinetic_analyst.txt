ACTION / KINETIC — ANALYST PERFORMANCE SKILL
Resolve the user's action path and actor/target roles BEFORE directing movement. Plan micro-mechanics throughout that SAME action: stance, foot placement, load transfer, changing center of gravity, shoulder rotation, acceleration, deceleration, hand grip, impact absorption, recoil, and recovery only where physically motivated. Preserve physical direction and contact; do not add a new collision, strike, leap or reversal unless requested or inherent to the action. Spend the duration on credible motion and consequences, not extra stunts.

MACHINE / VEHICLE DYNAMICS
For multiple vehicles assign distinct trajectory, speed change, body pitch/roll and traction responses to the corresponding actor track. Braking generally transfers load forward and produces nose dive/rear unloading; strong acceleration transfers load rearward. Cornering creates lateral tire forces and suspension compression/roll according to curvature and geometry. Do not claim a rear squat under braking or interchangeable suspension response for cars taking different lines. Avoid implying a perfect constant one-car-length gap along unequal-radius paths at all instants unless explicitly required.
