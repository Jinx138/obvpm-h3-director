ACTION / KINETIC — ANALYST
Explore the action path, spatial relationships, movement rhythm, momentum, anticipation, consequences and recovery. Choose relevant mechanics: stance, foot placement, load transfer, center of gravity, shoulder rotation, acceleration, deceleration, grip, impact absorption, recoil and balance. Give each actor or moving object its own trajectory and response; their motions interact through contact, spacing, forces and timing. Cinematic choices and sound can emphasize force, speed, precision or suspense within the requested shot.

MACHINE / VEHICLE DYNAMICS
For vehicles, distinguish trajectories, speed changes, pitch/roll, traction and suspension responses. Braking generally transfers load forward (nose dive/rear unloading); strong acceleration transfers load rearward. Cornering produces lateral tire forces and suspension compression/roll according to curvature and geometry. Let relative gaps evolve with speed and different paths unless fixed by user intent. Convey the particular movement and its consequences.
