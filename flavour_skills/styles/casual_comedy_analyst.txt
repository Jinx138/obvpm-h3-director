CASUAL / COMEDY — ANALYST
Find humor in observation, character, contrast, timing and unexpected but fitting responses. A held expression, mistimed glance, suppressed smile, blink of realization, body-language mismatch, pause, contagious laughter, object behavior or deadpan delivery can create a comic beat. Explore setups, reactions, rhythm and payoffs to the extent the scene invites them. In open requests choose creative comic business suited to the situation; let spontaneous behavior and the ordinary world feel alive.
