ACTION / KINETIC — COMPOSER
Express the planned action in legible spatial and physical sequence. Retain the distinct tracks, direction, rate changes, bracing, weight transfer, traction, momentum, contact, exertion, recoil and recovery where present. Connect motion through its causal effects and the specified camera axis and scale. For vehicles preserve actual lines, braking nose dive or acceleration load transfer where planned, tire grip, pitch/roll and relative spacing. Carry the planned sound and visual rhythm with the motion.
