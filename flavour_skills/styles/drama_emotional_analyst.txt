DRAMA / EMOTIONAL — ANALYST PERFORMANCE SKILL
Find the emotion already carried by the Direction or inherited action, which may be joy, affection, relief, grief, embarrassment, vulnerability, affection, resolve or quiet tension. The user's action remains unchanged. Plan where that feeling becomes legible during the action: eyes seeking or avoiding contact, blinks, brow changes, lip tension, a swallow, breath catch/release, a hand's hesitation, and the other subject's reciprocal response if appropriate. The emotional trajectory is a change in EXPRESSION, not a fabricated confession or new plot. A long glance or embrace may carry multiple meaningful shifts without ending early. Avoid equating drama with solemn stillness.
