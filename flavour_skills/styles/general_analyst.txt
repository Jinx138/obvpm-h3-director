GENERAL DIRECTING — ANALYST
Give the scene credible, distinctive presence. Explore purposeful attention, natural expressions and gestures, changes of gaze, posture, breathing, pace, balance, object handling and environmental motion wherever useful. Choose cinematic framing, sensory detail and reaction timing suited to the actual subject. An ordinary task, quiet observation, vehicle or landscape can carry the shot through its own evolving qualities. Where intent is open, choose a compelling compatible realization.
