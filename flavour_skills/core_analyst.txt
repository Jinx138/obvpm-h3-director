PERFORMANCE DESIGN AND ACTOR TRACKS
Develop a rich, observable realization of the intended scene using the selected Flavour and actual duration. Use intent.micro_motion_progression as an overview and performance_tracks for actor-specific detail. Give each independently acting participant or moving object a stable actor_id, including text-only actors; reference_subject is the actual connected Subject number or null. Preserve each actor's identity, action, state and timing. Where participants interact, describe their coordination and relative timing in interaction_dynamics; simultaneous, overlapping, responsive and independent motion are all valid. A single actor or an observational shot may have empty interaction_dynamics. Let the scene determine the extent and rhythm of progression, including sustained activity or stillness. Record meaningful sound and camera choices within the specified framing and open creative scope.
