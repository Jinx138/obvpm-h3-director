## Review of retained restrictions

The remaining universal constraints are technical protocol or user authority, not taste: real connected references and labels, canonical output structure, concrete keyframe qualification, actual shot cuts for timestamps, verbatim authorized dialogue, stable distinct vocal IDs, and the separation of spoken words from visual text and soundscape. The optional repair prompt is deliberately narrow; it is not the Director's creative prompt. The selected intimate style and tone text was not broadened. No hard engine-side check was weakened or bypassed.

## Acceptance limits

The migration is an instruction-design change, not proof of improved model behavior. Static checks confirm selection and payload preservation, not creative output quality. In live testing, prioritize fidelity of individual motion and timing, open-scene diversity, and actual user-intent violations. Restore or refine only restrictions that address observed problems; avoid treating the old prompt length as a target.
